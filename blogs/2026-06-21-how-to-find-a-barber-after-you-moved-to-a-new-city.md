Moving to a new city means leaving behind a barber you trust, which can make finding a new one feel more stressful than most people expect. Instead of picking the closest shop, take the time to compare barbers, check out their work, and try a few before settling on someone. The right barber isn't just giving you a great haircut—they become part of your routine and one of the people who helps a new city start feeling like home.
